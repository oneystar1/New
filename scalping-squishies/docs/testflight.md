# TestFlight build 1: tester notes

Paste the first block into App Store Connect → TestFlight → "What to Test".
The second block is the day-3 check-in to send every tester.

## What to Test (build 1)

Scalping Squishies is an idle game about flipping squishy fidget toys.
You tap a shelf, buy runs at stores, hire runners, and camp restock
lines against moms, kids and sneaker bros. This is the first outside
build. Nothing is final; the economy is a bot's guess.

Please try, in this order:

1. Play the first 10 minutes without reading anything. Note the first
   moment you were confused and what you did about it.
2. Camp a restock line (Hunt tab → Camp). Answer the events. Then open
   the doors and grab. Tell us if the 45-second wait felt long, short,
   or right.
3. Watch one boost video (Shelf → Free pull, or Hustle). Did it play?
   Did the reward arrive?
4. Open the Shop and buy the Starter Hustle Pack with your sandbox
   account (you will not be charged). Then force-quit the app, reopen,
   and check it is still owned. Then Settings → Restore purchases.
5. Put the phone down for an hour and come back. Did a notification
   arrive? Was the offline cash right?
6. Dohlidays is on from November 20. Check the Hunt tab for the season
   card and try to buy one seasonal item with Candy Canes.

Send feedback from Boost tab → ⚙️ Settings → Send feedback. It
pre-fills your device id so we can find your save.

Known in build 1: no sound in the camp scene yet; the leaderboard needs
the backend, which may be empty; ads are Google test ads.

## Day-3 questions

Three questions, answered in one message:

1. Where did you stop playing, and why?
2. What was the most confusing thing in the first ten minutes?
3. What, if anything, did you want to buy, and what stopped you?

## What we watch

From the dashboard (`npm run dashboard`): day-1 and day-7 return,
rewarded views per daily player, starter pack conversion by day 3. From
the inbox: anything two testers say independently.
