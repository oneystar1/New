Common questions

I bought something and didn't get it

Settings → Restore purchases first. Everything you own on your Apple ID comes back, including Remove Ads and VIP. If a Clout pack is still missing, send us the receipt and your device id and we will grant it by hand.

My save is gone

If cloud save was on, open the game on the same device and wait a few seconds on the title screen; the newer cloud save loads automatically. You can also paste a save code from Settings → Export on your old device. Hard reset wipes everything and asks twice.

I can't find my messages

Buyer messages are in the Deals tab. The badge on the tab counts unread offers. Offers expire, so a message that disappears was either answered or timed out.

Where are the ads?

The web version has no ads. On iOS, rewarded ads are optional boosts you choose to watch; interstitials play between restock runs unless you own Remove Ads or VIP.

How do I cancel VIP?

VIP is an Apple subscription. iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Scalping Squishies → Cancel. You keep the perks until the current week ends.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete cloud data removes everything on our server. Delete the app or clear site data to remove the local save. Details in the privacy policy.
