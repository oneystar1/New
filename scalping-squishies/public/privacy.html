Privacy Policy

Scalping Squishies · effective 25 October 2026 · Support

Scalping Squishies is an idle game. It works without an account, without your name, and without your email. This page says exactly what the game stores and who else sees anything.

What the game stores on your device

Your save file (cash, collection, settings, progress) and a random device id. On the web this lives in your browser's local storage; in the iOS app it lives in the app's own storage. Deleting the app or clearing site data removes it.

What is sent to our server

Only when a server is configured for the build you are playing (the game runs fully offline otherwise):

We never receive your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or payment details. The leaderboard handle is whatever you type; please don't type personal information into it.

Ads (iOS app)

The iOS app shows ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional and only play when you tap to watch one. Interstitials run between restock runs unless you buy Remove Ads or VIP. On first launch iOS asks whether you allow tracking; say no and you get non-personalized ads and the same game. Google's handling of ad data is described in Google's privacy policy and AdMob's data notice. The web version shows no third-party ads.

Purchases (iOS app)

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. We never see your card. RevenueCat validates receipts and tells our server what you bought, identified only by the random device id. Subscriptions (VIP Flipper) renew through Apple and can be cancelled in your Apple ID settings. Mystery pulls use in-game Clout; the pull odds are shown in the game before every pull.

Children

The game is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has sent us something personal through the leaderboard handle, contact us and we will delete it.

Delete your data

In the game: Boost tab → ⚙️ Settings → Delete cloud data. That removes your save, events, scores and purchase records from our server immediately and signs the device out. Local data is removed by deleting the app or clearing site data. Purchases stay restorable through Apple as long as your Apple ID owns them.

Your rights

You can ask us to export or delete anything above by contacting us. Because the only identifier is a random id stored on your device, include the id from Settings so we can find it. We answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the game shows a notice on the next launch and the date at the top of this page updates.
