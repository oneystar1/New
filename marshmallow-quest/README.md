# Marshmallow Quest

A 16-bit top-down rescue adventure in the spirit of *A Link to the Past*.
You are Puff, a marshmallow. Fire monsters carried your five buddies off to
Ember Keep. Get too close to fire and you toast; toast all the way and you melt.

Single self-contained HTML file, no build step. Open `index.html` over HTTP or
play the published page.

## Controls

| Action        | Keyboard                 | Touch / Gamepad          |
|---------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Move          | Arrows or WASD           | Joystick / left stick    |
| Sword, talk   | Z, J or Space            | A                        |
| Fire Spear    | X or K                   | B                        |
| Roll          | C, L or Shift            | ROLL / X                 |
| Pause, map    | Enter or Esc             | START                    |
| Mute          | M                        |                          |

## The world

- **Donut Village**: three donut houses. The Elder gives you the sword; the
  Baker and Granny heal you and hand out side quests (deliver a honey donut,
  find Nibs' lost sprinkle).
- **Toasty Woods**: Embers, fire bats, fire slimes, a lava snail, cuttable
  bushes hiding glaze jars, and Fluff in a wooden cage.
- **Cinder Cave**: lava, the Magma Toad mid-boss, and the Fire Spear.
- **Ember Keep**: a flame-vent corridor, a block-and-switch puzzle, a brazier
  door, torch statues, Torch Knights, three flame cages and the two-phase
  Pyre Lord.

Progress saves to the browser automatically. Choose Normal or Hard on the
title screen.
